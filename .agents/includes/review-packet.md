Review packet: `review_mode=pre_pr|requested`, repo, one or more issue URLs (or `none` when the criteria come from the request), the acceptance criteria, the remote ref, the immediate base SHA, the pushed head SHA, a worktree path that already has the objects, and the reviewer's own `Policy:` line are required. It also carries the accepted decisions (or `none`) and, in `pre_pr` mode, a changed-file summary from `git diff --stat <base>..<head>`, the local check results with the SHA they ran at, and, for a branch stacked on another PR, that PR with the head SHA it was verified at. A re-review packet adds the previous round's head SHA and its findings verbatim.
