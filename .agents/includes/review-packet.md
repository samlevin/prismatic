Review packet: `review_mode=pre_pr|requested`, repo, one or more issue URLs (or `none` when the criteria come from the request), the acceptance criteria for this layer, the remote ref, the immediate base SHA, the pushed head SHA, a worktree path that already has the objects, and the reviewer's own `Policy:` line are required. It also carries the accepted decisions (or `none`), a changed-file summary from `git diff --stat <base>..<head>` that names the behavior and the relevant callers or tests, the relevant validation results with the SHA they ran at (or which checks did not run), and verified prerequisites with their SHAs (or `none`). A re-review packet adds the previous round's base and head SHAs (its `Base/Head:` line) and its complete report verbatim.
